# ☕ Chloe & Co Full of Beans — Brand Audit & Digital Presence Analysis

A marketing and digital brand audit project focused on evaluating the online presence, customer experience, and discoverability of Chloe & Co Full of Beans — a community-driven café and social enterprise in Dundalk, Ireland.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project explored how branding, storytelling, and digital visibility influence customer engagement for small local businesses and social enterprises.

The audit combined:

- Brand strategy analysis
- Customer experience evaluation
- Social media audit
- Google discoverability review
- Competitor benchmarking
- Qualitative stakeholder interviewing
- Digital marketing recommendations

---

## 🔍 Key Findings

- Strong emotional and community-driven brand identity
- Limited Google presence and discoverability
- Instagram presence active but low reach
- No searchable online menu on Google
- Inconsistent promotional content strategy
- Strong in-store customer experience and visual branding

---

## 📊 Competitor Benchmarking

| Business | Instagram Followers | Google Reviews | Menu Online | Posting Frequency |
|----------|---------------------|----------------|-------------|------------------|
| Chloe & Co | 141 | 8 | No | Weekly |
| Competitor A | 1.2K | 120 | Yes | Daily |
| Competitor B | 950 | 85 | Yes | 3x weekly |

---

## 🎯 Recommendations

- Improve Google Business optimisation
- Add searchable menu and updated photos
- Increase short-form video content
- Develop consistent weekly content plan
- Use storytelling-focused campaigns
- Encourage customer reviews and engagement

---

## 🧠 Personal Reflection

One of the most impactful parts of this project was interviewing the owner and learning about her long-term involvement in charity and community support work in Dundalk.

Understanding the emotional mission behind the café added depth to the audit process and highlighted how authentic storytelling can become a powerful branding tool.

This project strengthened my interest in combining marketing, customer psychology, and digital analytics to support meaningful businesses.
